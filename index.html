THE EVENT MODEL

Designed for access.

Remote competition

Competitors can participate in approved events from their own locations where the official format allows it.

THE DOCUMENTED TIMELINE

A developing legacy.

2025
INITIAL EDITION

Greenwick 2025 announced

The Commonwealth of Greenwick announced an MSC Games programme for November 2025. Reports later recorded insufficient participation in parts of the programme and a postponement to January 2026.

2026
FORMAT TRANSITION

New host sought; online approach announced

Following the withdrawal of Rondland as host, the MSC committee publicly described a move to fully online competition and sought a replacement host nation.

NEXT
TO BE CONFIRMED

Future editions & host interest

A historical call for prospective hosts referenced 2033, 2036 and 2039. The 2026 organiser update also sought a replacement virtual host. No future host, schedule or opening date is presented here as confirmed.

OUR PURPOSE

Built for the
entire community.

MSC provides a framework for shared sporting opportunities regardless of a micronation’s size, recognition or physical borders, subject to published event eligibility and competition rules.

01

Fair play

Accessible standards, credible results, and clear expectations for competitors and organisers.

02

Inclusion

Formats that make participation realistic for small and geographically dispersed micronations.

03

Cooperation

Bringing national representatives together through shared experiences and friendly competition.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT

Our roots in Greenwick.

The Commonwealth of Greenwick announced MSC Games plans in 2025. Later postponements and hosting changes prompted organisers to explore new arrangements for participation and administration. The MSC website distinguishes these documented developments from future programmes that have not been formally confirmed.

PRIVACY & INFORMATION

Your data matters.

When the live database is configured, application data is stored in the MSC project's Supabase database and limited to authorised administrators by row-level security. Submitted information is used to review expressions of interest and coordinate relevant Games administration. Do not submit identification documents, financial details or sensitive personal information through these forms. Contact MSC administration to request access, correction or deletion of an application. The portal does not claim any connection to the International Olympic Committee.
